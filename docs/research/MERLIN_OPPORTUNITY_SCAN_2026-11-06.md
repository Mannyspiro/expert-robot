### Insurance licensing gate — Arizona
- Arizona DIFI explains that both individuals and business entities can be insurance producers; when an established business entity name is used in insurance transactions, the entity must also hold an insurance license. Official guidance: https://difi.az.gov/licensing/insurance-professionals
- Disposition: PROCUREMENT READINESS GATE. For each insurance solicitation, separately verify the individual producer license, agency/entity license, applicable lines, carrier appointments, and scope required. Do not infer entity licensing from the user's individual P&C license.

## Merlin data-quality and safety rules
1. Keep lead status distinct: OPEN SOLICITATION, FORECAST, INCUMBENT, GRANT CYCLE, PARTNER APPLICATION, WATCHLIST, CLOSED, and UNVERIFIED.
2. Store source URL, source owner, access date, deadline/time zone, source freshness, jurisdiction, eligibility evidence, and next verification date.
3. Never infer WOSB/SBE certification, citizenship, agency licensing, carrier appointments, SAM registration, nonprofit status, or grant eligibility.
4. The user's Arizona P&C producer license does not alone establish agency/entity licensing, appointments, or authority in another state.
5. Do not transmit identity attributes, credentials, financial records, tax IDs, or agency documents to a third party without explicit approval.
6. Luxury authorization must be verified at the brand, territory, channel, and product level. Distinguish authorized dealer, authorized wholesale retailer, distributor, licensee, reseller, and authenticated pre-owned seller.
7. No application submission, vendor registration, purchase, deposit, exclusivity commitment, or contract acceptance without explicit user approval.
8. Every live lead should be independently rechecked before being presented as currently open.
