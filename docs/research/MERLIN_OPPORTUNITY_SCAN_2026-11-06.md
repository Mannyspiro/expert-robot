## Arizona procurement

### Arizona Procurement Portal (APP)
- Official entry point: https://az.gov/government/doing-business-state
- Vendor registration is free. Suppliers must complete both registration parts, select applicable commodity codes, and submit offers through APP. APP sends notifications matching selected codes.
- Merlin action: create a registration-readiness checklist; do not claim the agency is registered until confirmed.
- Source: Arizona.gov, "Doing Business With The State" (accessed 2026-11-06).

### City of Tucson Small Business Enterprise
- Official page: https://www.tucsonaz.gov/Departments/Business-Services-Department/Procurement/Contract-Compliance-DBE-SBE/Small-Business-Enterprise
- Program may provide bid preferences and subcontracting goals for eligible businesses.
- Published criteria include owner citizenship/permanent-resident status, at-risk owner investment, applicable size/revenue standards, Pima County business address of record, for-profit status, owner control and expertise, personal-net-worth ceiling, required licenses, and City vendor registration.
- Disposition: CONDITIONAL. Verify every criterion; do not infer qualification from woman ownership, a producer license, or Tucson-area residence.
- Source: City of Tucson, "Small Business Enterprise" (accessed 2026-11-06).

## Amateur radio / technology / community grants

### Amateur Radio Digital Communications (ARDC)
- Official page: https://www.ardc.net/apply/
- 2027 deadlines listed: January 15, April 1, July 1, and September 1 (scholarship applications also due September 1). Grants generally go to eligible organizations, usually U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofits or international equivalents, and require open access.
- Priority themes include amateur radio growth, education, and research & development.
- Disposition: PARTNER ROUTE. Develop a public-benefit digital communications, STEM education, or open-access amateur-radio project with an eligible organization/fiscal sponsor. Do not treat an individual operator or ordinary for-profit agency as automatically eligible.
- Source: ARDC, "Apply for a Grant" (accessed 2026-11-06).

### Arizona Radio Club of America (ARCA)
- Official page: https://arca-az.org/programs
- Lists scholarships for Arizona amateur-radio operators studying electronics, communications, or STEM; club grants for ARCA-affiliated clubs; no upcoming deadlines displayed at time of scan.
- Disposition: WATCH NEXT CYCLE; verify current rules and club affiliation with ARCA.
- Source: ARCA Programs (accessed 2026-11-06).

## Retail / luxury brand partner pathways

These are exploratory commercial leads. Wholesale account approval is not equivalent to exclusive dealership, distribution rights, or manufacturer authorization across every brand in a supplier's catalog.

### Tresor Collection
- Partner page: https://tresorcollection.com/pages/new-store-opening
- Describes wholesale pricing and partner access; says verified boutiques, jewelry retailers, concept stores, or online retailers with an active business license may apply. It reports a 3–7 working-day review period.

- Disposition: WHOLESALE SUPPLIER LEAD, NOT PROOF OF DIRECT MANUFACTURER APPOINTMENT. Require brand/SKU-specific provenance and written channel permissions.

## Merlin data-quality and safety rules
1. Keep lead status distinct: OPEN SOLICITATION, FORECAST, INCUMBENT, GRANT CYCLE, PARTNER APPLICATION, WATCHLIST, CLOSED, and UNVERIFIED.
2. Store source URL, source owner, access date, deadline/time zone, source freshness, jurisdiction, eligibility evidence, and next verification date.
3. Never infer WOSB/SBE certification, citizenship, agency licensing, carrier appointments, SAM registration, nonprofit status, or grant eligibility.
4. The user's Arizona P&C producer license does not alone establish agency/entity licensing, appointments, or authority in another state.
5. Do not transmit identity attributes, credentials, financial records, tax IDs, or agency documents to a third party without explicit approval.
6. Luxury authorization must be verified at the brand, territory, channel, and product level. Distinguish authorized dealer, authorized wholesale retailer, distributor, licensee, reseller, and authenticated pre-owned seller.
7. No application submission, vendor registration, purchase, deposit, exclusivity commitment, or contract acceptance without explicit user approval.
8. Every live lead should be independently rechecked before being presented as currently open.
