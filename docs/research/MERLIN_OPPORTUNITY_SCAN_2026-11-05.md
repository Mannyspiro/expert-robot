## Confidence and cautions

This scan does not establish that the agency has an active SAM.gov registration,
a qualifying NAICS size standard, WOSB/EDWOSB certification, the ownership
percentages required by a program, or any insurance appointment beyond the
known Arizona P&C scope. The WOSB pathway is a possible avenue to verify, not
a claim of qualification. Individual scholarship fit remains conditional on
the program's enrollment, degree, GPA, citizenship, financial-need, and
application rules. Do not submit applications, certifications, bids, or
personal documents without the user's explicit approval.

No specific currently open government insurance-broker solicitation was
confirmed in this first scan. The next procurement pass should inspect live
SAM.gov and Arizona/Pima notices plus the Pima County buying-plan detail.
