# Merlin unified research program — 2026-11-04

This program consolidates the active mathematical, physics, xenomathematics, superorganism and engineering threads into one evidence-gated workflow. It is a research plan, not a claim that the listed open questions have already been solved.

## Operating loop

1. **Discover:** use the academic spider and public scholarly metadata to identify primary sources, authors, definitions and existing results.
2. **Normalize:** add source provenance, topic labels, work/researcher graph edges and exact bibliographic identifiers.
3. **Enumerate:** use ideonomy to generate alternative representations, coordinate systems, invariants and candidate mechanisms.
4. **Separate:** keep sourced facts, assumptions, conjectures, counterexamples, experiments and formal theorems in distinct ledger records.
5. **Falsify:** search for counterexamples and boundary cases before trying to prove or simulate a claim.
6. **Compute:** run bounded, reproducible symbolic/numerical experiments with pinned inputs and resource budgets.
7. **Formalize:** turn precise mathematical statements into Lean theorems with explicit assumptions, dependency audits and reproducible Avrea CI receipts.
8. **Engineer:** map only sufficiently specified models into shared CAD/electrical IR, simulation and design artifacts.
9. **Review:** require source, proof, simulation and engineering evidence appropriate to each claim; no automatic authority expansion.

## Research tracks

### A. THTG / UFT mathematical reconstruction

Priority tasks:
- Recover the complete action, matter action, boundary terms and source equations from primary material.
- Keep the reported Page-curve formula separate from generalized entropy/island prescriptions.
- Derive the five-dimensional curvature-squared invariant basis under explicit field-redefinition, boundary and symmetry assumptions.
- Reconcile coupling dimensions with the action normalization.
- Recover the exact FRG regulator, gauge fixing, ghost operators and projection prescription.
- Derive beta functions independently from that setup; do not tune coefficients to preserve a proposed fixed point.
- Recompute fixed-point coordinates and the stability matrix with interval/error analysis only after the flow equations are fixed.

Current formal results:
- A continuous real-valued function on a nonempty compact interval attains a maximum (conditional theorem).
- The four beta functions transcribed from the THTG report, with decimal coefficients treated as exact rationals, have no simultaneous fixed point with positive (g).
- The displayed pre-Page expression is under active Lean qualification for strict monotonicity and absence of an interior maximum.
- None of these theorem-level results validates the physical action or establishes that the report's beta functions are the correct FRG flow.

### B. Xenomathematics and alternative symbolic systems

Explore mathematics without assuming conventional human notation is fundamental:
- Typed glyphs as operators, operands, transformations, constraints and composable programs.
- Formal grammars and operational semantics for glyph systems; define equality, composition, inverses and rewrite rules before assigning meaning.
- Multiple coordinate systems attached to one object simultaneously: Cartesian, polar, cylindrical, spherical, barycentric, homogeneous/projective, curvilinear and local frame coordinates.
- Algebraic number systems: complex, split-complex, dual numbers, quaternions, dual quaternions, octonions and Clifford/geometric algebras.
- Extend carefully to 3D/4D/5D representations, distinguishing algebraic dimension, geometric dimension and embedding dimension.
- Compare eigenvalues, spectra and invariant subspaces only where the relevant algebra and operator definitions make those concepts well-defined.

Deliverables:
- A machine-readable glyph/operator grammar.
- Conversion laws and invariant-preservation tests across coordinate systems.
- Small Lean-certified algebraic laws for each new operator family.
- Visual/CAD renderers that consume the same typed representation rather than inventing separate semantics.

### C. Superorganism mathematics

Model collective systems as explicit graphs and dynamical systems:
- Agent/resource/task graphs, local interaction rules and global objective functions.
- Distributed optimization, consensus, network flows and information-theoretic measures.
- Emergent phase changes, stability, synchronization and multi-scale feedback.
- Compare ant-colony, microbial, neural and engineered swarms only through sourced, operational definitions.
- Search for invariants and failure of scaling laws rather than treating analogy as proof.

### D. Geometry, spacetime and time-dependent structures

- Build a common transformation layer for affine, projective, Euclidean and non-Euclidean geometries.
- Represent 2D/3D/4D/5D coordinates as typed spaces with explicit maps, metrics and units.
- Track frame changes separately from physical time evolution.
- Treat block-spacetime projections and time-crystal models as mathematical/physical hypotheses with explicit assumptions and observables.
- Use eigenvalues, tensors, differential forms and geometric algebra where their domains and invariance properties are specified.

### E. Engineering realization

- Continue from shared Engineering IR, hierarchical frames and physical netlist endpoints.
- Add multiple component instances with stable instance IDs.
- Add mechanical joints, constraint graphs, degrees of freedom and kinematics.
- Add harness paths and connector/pin libraries, then KiCad adapters and native ERC/DRC.
- Connect simulation outputs back to hypothesis evidence, with reproducible inputs and model hashes.

## Promotion rules

- A source citation supports provenance, not truth.
- A numerical result supports only its stated model, precision and parameter range.
- A Lean proof certifies only its exact proposition and assumptions.
- A proof receipt must bind to the source hash, commit, workflow run and audited axiom set.
- A CAD/netlist consistency check is not safety certification or fabrication approval.
- Keep speculative glyphs, xenomathematical systems and physics conjectures marked as proposed until operational definitions and falsifiers exist.
