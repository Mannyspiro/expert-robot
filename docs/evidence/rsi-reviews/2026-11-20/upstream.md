## Primary research

1. **New within the window.** Jayaram and colleagues submitted *How Much Should an Agent Remember?*, arXiv:2611.26361v1, on **Nov 19 at 16:29:16 UTC**. Their AppWorld experiments found that optimal guideline volume varied by model. They also traced a consolidation error that dropped downstream selection metadata. The authors separately warn that repeated observations can reinforce erroneous advice. For Merlin, retain every eligible trace reference, vary the guidance actually delivered, and interleave repeated baseline runs to estimate variance. The paper uses CC BY 4.0. Its experiments predate today's library release. Coverage includes submission history, measurement setting, provenance and variance sections. [Paper and methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.26361v1) · [Submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26361v1)
2. **Newly reviewed, before the window.** Yang and colleagues submitted *Forget Freely, Grep Later*, arXiv:2611.25123v1, on **Nov 18 at 20:02:57 UTC**. Their persistent log and on-demand search provide a simple challenger to memory compression. Results depend on the task. In the reported comparisons, MemGPT outscored GrepMem on ContextBench key–value recall, whereas GrepMem led on ContextBench log recall. Compare on both exact-ID lookup and later-disclosed questions, using only a consented, pinned corpus and the same retrieval allowance. Coverage includes the version history, method and comparison table. [Paper and results](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.25123v1) · [Submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25123v1)
3. **Newly reviewed, before the window.** Zhang and colleagues submitted *AutoRAGBench*, arXiv:2611.25582v1, on **Nov 19 at 06:26:28 UTC**. They evaluated system-development agents on nine tasks and averaged main scores across five held-out answering models. That design motivates a transfer check for retrieval changes across answering models. Separate development cost, passage retrieval and cited final decisions. Coverage includes the abstract, evaluation outline, cost section and main results table. [Paper and evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.25582v1) · [Submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25582v1)
