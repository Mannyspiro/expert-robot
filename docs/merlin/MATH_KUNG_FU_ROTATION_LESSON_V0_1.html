One rotation, four mathematical languages

Rotate the point (1,0) around the origin. Change the angle and watch four descriptions of the same motion update together.

Matrix

R(θ) = [[cos θ, −sin θ], [sin θ, cos θ]]

Complex numbers

z′ = (cos θ + i sin θ)z

Quaternions

For rotation about the z-axis, q = cos(θ/2) + k sin(θ/2), and v′ = qvq⁻¹.

Geometric algebra

With B = e₁e₂ and B² = −1, rotor R = cos(θ/2) − B sin(θ/2), and v′ = RvR̃.
