Symmetry in motion: make a mathematical artwork

Start with one small shape. Repeat it by rotating around a center. You are both the artist choosing the visual language and the mathematician investigating the rule.

Try a hand-drawn version too: mark a center, divide a circle into equal angles, and repeat one motif.

What is the mathematical structure?

With n identical motifs arranged evenly, one step is 360° ÷ n. Rotating the whole design by that angle maps it onto itself. Repeating the rotation generates a cyclic symmetry group, written Cn, for this idealized design.

The artwork is not the group: the group describes transformations that preserve the pattern. A change to the motif or its placement can break some symmetries.

Try predicting: if you choose 12 motifs, how many degrees are in one smallest positive rotational step?
