# After the Grid Went Violet

## Series premise

Beneath a city that has grown used to violet power rationing, Iona Vale
and Ren Sato keep an obsolete relay alive. The two engineers inherit a
maintenance log that blames hardware for every failure. When their own
test contradicts the log, they start to ask who defines what counts as
evidence. Their friendship changes as each learns to challenge the
other's most comfortable explanation.

Iona notices physical patterns and makes decisions under pressure. She
tends to close an investigation after finding a practical fix. Ren is
good at reading instruments and keeping a record. He sometimes wants
one more test when the job calls for a decision. Technician Mara
Keene joins in chapter six. Mara can identify a wiring fault by the way
a room behaves, but her documentation has to convince people who were
never in that room. Mara's face design and voice are awaiting casting.

## Twelve-chapter series

The ten-minute target for each chapter gives the season a planned
runtime of two hours. An episode usually moves from a two-minute
story setup to a three-minute investigation and three-minute visual
test, then uses its final two minutes for a decision and its consequences.
Vary this rhythm when the story asks for it.

## Pilot acceptance frame

- Compare one path with two independent equal paths, total power 6 W,
  each active path 4 K/W and sink 20 °C. Calculate steady-state rise
  from the stipulated linear relation. Hold total input and boundary
  conditions constant.
- The predicted tile temperatures are 44 °C and 32 °C. Unequal contact
  resistance or throttling could confound a physical comparison. A
  steady-state result inconsistent with the equal split falsifies at
  least one model assumption under the stated conditions.
- Apply a continuum approximation for the thermal-field chapters where
  a refinement test can measure error. Consider n-ality as independent
  design axes for the chip comparisons. Defer simplex coordinates for
  the single branch comparison. Fuzzy memberships may rank candidate
  imagery or design options while scientific acceptance uses explicit
  tests and independent review.
- The Lean source formalizes equal division in an ideal rational
  model. The Blender shots illustrate the design. Heat-flow values are
  hand-calculated in the pilot and can be independently reproduced from
  the stated assumptions. No physical measurements are represented.
