04 / THE PRACTICE OF SCIENCE

What makes good inquiry?

Scientific character is demonstrated in habits, methods and institutions that make correction possible.

01

Separate knowing from guessing

Mark observations, assumptions, interpretations, hypotheses and conjectures distinctly.

02

Invite disconfirmation

Seek counterexamples, alternative explanations, negative results and independent replication.

03

Show your work

Preserve instruments, data, code, calculations, editions, methods and source provenance.

04

Understand the context

Study institutions, predecessors, collaborators, resources and the questions of each era.

05

Calibrate confidence

State uncertainty, limits, scope and what new evidence would change the conclusion.

06

Give fair credit

Look beyond lone-genius stories and recover collective, cross-cultural and overlooked work.

04B / ENGINEERING AND EPISTEMIC GOVERNANCE

Plan before building. Test before believing.

Merlin uses a mandatory task plan, explicit scope, multiple reasoning modes, bias checks and bounded resources.

01

Specify the work

Define the objective, non-goals, requirements, acceptance tests, dependencies and interfaces before creating artifacts.

02

Map the scope

Assign one primary domain, justified secondary domains and named subsystems. Flag mis-scoped or ambiguous items for review.

03

Reason in both directions

Use induction for patterns, deduction for consequences of stated premises, and abduction to compare explanations.

04

Audit assumptions and bias

Check fallacies, cognitive and design biases, confounders, selection, measurement, incentives and missing alternatives.

05

Try to disprove it

State counterexamples, negative controls, sensitivity checks and evidence that would change the conclusion.

06

Budget and verify

Set time, memory, disk and input limits. Test, independently review, preserve provenance and define rollback.

Operational rule: research outputs remain candidates until reviewed. The plan validator and CI checks are implemented; runtime-wide enforcement is a separate integration gate.

05 / THE UNMAPPED

Disciplines not yet named

Merlin can propose new fields, but novelty alone is not a discovery. A candidate needs precise objects, formal concepts, methods, testable questions and independent scrutiny.
